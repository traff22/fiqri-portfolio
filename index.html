Editing is most of my work. Motion graphics and design support it.

Video editing

YouTube long-form and short-form ads. I cut for pacing first, then do the sound design, the colour grade and the exports each platform needs.

FQ / THE EDITING ROOMON THE TIMELINE

A desk, two monitors, a timeline, and one more cut.

On the timeline. Cut by cut, finding the rhythm.

I'm Fiqri, a video editor and motion graphics designer. When I cut, I'm thinking about what the viewer will feel at each edit.

I've worked on storytelling pieces, promos, motion visuals and cinematic edits with production teams in Indonesia, and remotely with a team in Singapore.

2024 to nowPT Astronacci International · Video Designer
2023 to 2024Logistics 9 Pte Ltd · Video Editor (Remote)
2020 to 2022CIO Academy · Video Editor & Graphic Designer

Working with AI

AI is part of my toolkit, not a stand-in for taste. It lets me generate footage that could never be filmed on the budget or the deadline: impossible establishing shots, extra B-roll, effects plates, and quick previs to test an idea before anyone picks up a camera.

It also takes the slow, technical passes: masks and rotoscoping, cleanup, upscaling, transcripts and rough subtitles.

That is how a video that once looked impossible gets made, and fast. The story, the timing and the final cut stay with me.
